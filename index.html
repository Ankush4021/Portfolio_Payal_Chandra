A little about me

I like making ideas look and feel like something.

I'm Payal, a graphic designer and video editor. I enjoy taking an idea and working out how it should come across - as a poster, a brand visual, a reel or a longer video.

My work moves between still design and moving images. I pay attention to the small things: the way type sits on a page, how a cut lands, and whether the final piece feels right for the people it's meant for.

I'm still learning with every project, trying new approaches and getting better at turning a rough brief into something clear and considered.
